Apple Pie Recipe

Description

This pie keeps it simple with a flaky butter or shortening-based crust and a lightly sweetened filling that’s spiced with cinnamon and thickened with flour. To make this pie, you’ll need:

ingredients:

  • All-purpose flour
  • Unsalted butter
  • Kosher salt
  • Tart apples

Steps

  1. Mix together flour, butter, and salt in a large bowl with your fingertips until butter is well incorporated
  2. Let 1 disk stand at room temperature about 5 minutes. Roll into a 12-inch circle and fit into a 9-inch pie plate. Chill.
  3. Preheat oven to 425°F with oven rack in lowest position. Stir together apples, sugar, flour, cinnamon, and salt.
  4. Roll remaining disk to a 12-inch circle. Cut into 12 strips and arrange in lattice pattern over apples. Trim and crimp edges.
